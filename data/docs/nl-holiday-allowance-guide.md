# Netherlands Holiday Allowance (Vakantiegeld) Guide

In the Netherlands, holiday allowance ("vakantiegeld" / vakantiebijslag) is a statutory payment
separate from Belgian holiday pay rules.

## Rate

The statutory minimum holiday allowance is **8% of gross annual salary**, accrued from June to May
and paid out in May or June.

## Part-time employees

Holiday allowance is proportional to actual earnings, so part-time employees automatically receive
8% of their own gross salary. No separate occupation-fraction calculation is required.

## Leaving mid-year

On termination, the accrued but unpaid holiday allowance (from the last pay-out month up to the
leaving date) is settled on the final payslip at the same 8% rate.

## Note

These rules apply to Dutch employment contracts only. Belgian "double holiday pay" (92% of monthly
salary) is a different concept and is **not** governed by this guide.

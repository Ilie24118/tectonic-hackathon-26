# The 30% Ruling (Netherlands)

The 30% ruling is a Dutch tax facility for eligible incoming employees ("expats") recruited from
abroad with specific expertise. When granted, up to **30% of gross salary** can be paid as a
tax-free allowance for extraterritorial costs.

Eligibility requires a valid decision from the Belastingdienst, a minimum taxable salary threshold,
and that the employee lived more than 150 km from the Dutch border before hiring. The maximum
duration was reduced to **5 years** for recent grants. Payroll must apply the ruling only for the
period stated on the decision.

# Reporting Sick Leave (Belgium)

## Notifying the employer

An employee who is unable to work due to illness must notify their manager **on the first day of
absence, within one working day**. Notification can be by phone or through the HR self-service portal.

## Medical certificate

A medical certificate is required from the **first day** of absence if the employer or CLA requests
it; otherwise from the second consecutive day. The certificate must reach HR within **two working
days** of the start of the absence.

## Guaranteed salary

White-collar employees are entitled to one month of guaranteed salary from the employer. Blue-collar
rules differ by joint committee.

## Recurrence

A relapse within 14 calendar days is treated as a continuation of the original absence, not a new one.

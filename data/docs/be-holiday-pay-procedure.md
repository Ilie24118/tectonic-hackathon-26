# Belgian Holiday Pay (Vakantiegeld) Procedure

This procedure covers the calculation of single and double holiday pay for employees under Belgian
employment law, including departures during the calendar year.

## Double holiday pay

Double holiday pay ("dubbel vakantiegeld") equals **92% of the gross monthly salary** for a full-time
employee, paid once per year, normally in May or June.

## Part-time employees

For part-time employees, double holiday pay is calculated **pro rata** based on the employee's average
occupation fraction during the vacation qualifying year (the "vakantiedienstjaar"), which is the
**calendar year preceding** the year in which the holiday is taken.

## Departure mid-year (exit holiday pay)

When an employee leaves mid-year, the employer must settle **departure holiday pay**
("vertrekvakantiegeld"). This covers:

1. Holiday pay accrued in the **previous** qualifying year but not yet taken.
2. Holiday pay accrued in the **current** year up to the departure date.

The reference period for the accrual is the **qualifying year (year N-1)** for already-vested rights,
and the **running current year (year N)** pro-rata for the current accrual. Double holiday pay on
departure is **7.67% of gross annual earnings** of the relevant period, adjusted by the part-time
occupation fraction.

## Process

- Confirm the employee's occupation fraction per month from the contract history.
- Apply the fraction to the 92% base for the vested portion.
- Settle on the final payslip; report to the social secretariat within the monthly cut-off.

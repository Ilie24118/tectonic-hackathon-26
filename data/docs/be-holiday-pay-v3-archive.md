# Belgian Holiday Pay Procedure (version 3 - archived)

Superseded by version 4. Retained for audit history.

Double holiday pay equals 92% of the gross monthly salary for a full-time employee. Part-time
employees are paid pro rata to their occupation fraction during the qualifying year.

On departure mid-year, settle the departure holiday pay covering vested rights from the previous
qualifying year plus current-year accrual. This version did not yet include the 2026 clarification
on averaging occupation fractions across multiple part-time contracts.

# Meal Vouchers (Maaltijdcheques)

Meal vouchers are a common Belgian benefit. The **maximum face value is 8.00 EUR** per effectively
worked day. The split is a minimum employer contribution up to 6.91 EUR and a minimum employee
contribution of 1.09 EUR.

To remain exempt from social security and tax, the vouchers must be granted per worked day, be
non-transferable, and the employee contribution of at least 1.09 EUR must be respected. Part-time
employees receive vouchers only for days effectively worked.

# Belgian Payroll Cut-off Dates 2026

All monthly variable inputs (overtime, absences, bonuses, contract changes, departures) must reach
the Payroll BE team by the **cut-off date** to be processed in the same month's run.

- Standard monthly cut-off: the **18th** of each month at 12:00.
- December cut-off is earlier: **12 December** due to year-end closing.
- Departures and final settlements should be submitted at least **3 working days before** the cut-off
  to allow calculation of departure holiday pay and premiums.

Inputs received after cut-off are processed in the following month's run with a correction.

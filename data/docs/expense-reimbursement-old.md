# Expense reimbursement (legacy guidelines)

Employees can claim business expenses via the expense tool. Submit receipts within 30 days. The
daily lump-sum allowance for domestic business trips and per-kilometre car allowance are set by
finance and updated periodically.

This document has no current owner and the amounts have not been refreshed since 2020.

# New Hire Onboarding Checklist (Belgium)

Before the start date:

- Register the employee in the Dimona declaration **before** the first working hour.
- Collect the signed employment contract and ID/residence documents.
- Register the employee with the social secretariat and the group insurance.
- Set up meal vouchers and any applicable mobility benefit.

Week one:

- Enrol in the occupational health service and schedule any required medical exam.
- Communicate the work regulations ("arbeidsreglement").
- Confirm bank details for salary payment.

The first payroll run for a new hire follows the standard monthly cut-off.

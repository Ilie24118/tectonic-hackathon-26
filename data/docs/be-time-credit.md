# Time Credit and Thematic Leave (Belgium)

Employees can reduce or suspend their working time through the federal time-credit system, subject
to seniority and the size of the employer.

Thematic leave includes parental leave, medical assistance leave, and palliative care leave. Each
has its own maximum duration and a monthly allowance paid by the RVA/ONEM. Parental leave can be
taken full-time, half-time, or one-fifth, up to **4 months full-time equivalent** per child.

Applications must respect the notice periods set out in the CLA and be registered with the social
secretariat before the leave starts.

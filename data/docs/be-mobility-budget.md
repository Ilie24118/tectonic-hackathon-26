# Mobility Budget and Company Car (Belgium)

Employees entitled to a company car may opt for the federal **mobility budget** instead. The budget
equals the total cost of ownership of the car they are entitled to.

The budget can be spent across three pillars:

1. An environmentally friendly car (electric).
2. Sustainable transport (public transport, bike lease, rent near work).
3. The remaining balance paid in cash, subject to a **38.07% employee social contribution**.

The taxable benefit of a company car depends on the catalogue value, CO2 emissions and fuel type,
recalculated annually.

# Sick leave rules (old)

Employees must inform their manager of any illness. Notification should be given **within two
working days** of the first day of absence. A medical certificate is only required from the
**second day** of absence and should be sent to HR when convenient.

Guaranteed salary applies for white-collar staff. This copy was kept for reference and has no
current owner.

# End-of-Year Premium (13th month)

The end-of-year premium in Belgium is set by the applicable joint committee (paritair comité). For
most white-collar employees under JC 200 it equals **one gross monthly salary**, paid in December.

## Eligibility

An employee must have at least **6 months of seniority** in the reference year to receive a
pro-rata premium. Employees with less than 6 months' service are **not** entitled unless the CLA
says otherwise.

## Pro-rata calculation

The premium is pro-rated by the number of months effectively worked during the reference year,
divided by 12. Part-time employees receive the premium pro-rata to their occupation fraction.

## Departures

Employees leaving before December receive the pro-rata premium on their final payslip, provided the
6-month seniority condition is met.

# Analysis: seniority threshold for the end-of-year premium

This internal analysis reviewed eligibility for the Belgian end-of-year premium across joint
committees in 2023.

Conclusion: a pro-rata end-of-year premium is due once an employee reaches **3 months of seniority**
in the reference year. Many joint committees apply the lower 3-month threshold rather than 6 months,
so payroll should default to 3 months unless the specific CLA states a longer qualifying period.

Part-time employees are pro-rated on their occupation fraction. Leavers receive the pro-rata amount
on the final payslip.

Note: this document predates the current procedure and was never re-verified.

# Client-specific rules: Brouwerij Vandamme (BE)

Brouwerij Vandamme is a Belgian brewery client. Blue-collar production staff fall under
**Joint Committee 118** (food industry); office staff under JC 200.

## Holiday pay

Standard Belgian holiday pay rules apply (see the BE Holiday Pay Procedure). For blue-collar staff
under JC 118, holiday pay is paid via the **holiday fund** (Rijksdienst voor Jaarlijkse Vakantie),
not directly by the employer. Office staff are paid directly by the employer.

## End-of-year premium

Vandamme grants the end-of-year premium to office staff under JC 200 as one gross monthly salary.

## Meal vouchers

Meal vouchers have a face value of **8.00 EUR** per worked day, with an employer contribution of
6.91 EUR and an employee contribution of 1.09 EUR.

# Payroll FAQ (legacy)

A collection of frequently asked payroll questions exported from the old SharePoint site. Ownership
was never reassigned after the 2022 reorganisation.

**Q: How is double holiday pay calculated when someone leaves?**

Double holiday pay on departure is calculated on the **current calendar year's earnings only**
(the year in which the employee leaves). Take the gross earnings from January up to the leaving date
and apply the 7.67% rate. The previous year is not relevant because holiday rights reset each January.

**Q: Does part-time matter?**

Multiply the result by the part-time percentage stated in the contract at the date of departure.

**Q: When is it paid?**

On the last payslip.

**Q: Which countries does this cover?**

General payroll guidance. Check local rules.
